about

This space is for you.

I created Somewhere Between because I learned the hard way that you can spend so much time trying to become better that you forget to take care of the person you're trying to be.

This is a place for the things we don't always know how to say — self-worth, perfectionism, opening up, trust, mental health, healing, and everything in between.

You don't have to earn your worth. You already have it.

what we believe

A few things we hold onto.

Honesty over performance

The real version of a feeling is more useful than the tidy one. We'd rather sit with the mess than perform having it together.

Progress, not perfection

Healing isn't linear, and it was never supposed to be. Small, imperfect steps still count.

You are not alone in this

Different struggles, different stories, same reminder — whatever you're carrying, someone else has carried something like it too.
