If you're in crisis right now

If you are in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services. These lines are free, confidential, and available around the clock.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline Call or text 988 (US) — 24/7 support for anyone in emotional distress.
Crisis Text Line Text HOME to 741741 — free, 24/7 support by text message.
National Alliance for Eating Disorders Call 1-866-662-1235 — helpline for eating disorder support.
